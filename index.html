About

Mambernetics is a widespread community of table-top gamers, cosplayers, and enthusiasts of geek culture. Our events bring people together to celebrate their passions and share their creativity.

The Big Event

We are excited to celebrate our Friendships, our Fandoms, and our other “Fs” at the biennial Mambernetics Dinner in Atlanta on Labor Day Weekend!

There is this other little local event that happens to be occurring that weekend, some kind of herpetological costume convention. Obviously, we’ll be drawing a lot of attention away from them, but by way of apology, we’re supporting their designated charity partner. See the Donate section elsewhere on this page for details.

Sponsorships

Mambernetics is always looking for sponsors to help us stage our events great and small. All kinds of sponsorships are welcome: donations of gaming space, design expertise, and direct financial support make what we do possible.

Do you have a skill or a resource that can help us advance the cause of gaming and geekery? Tell us about it!
